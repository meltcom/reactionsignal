### Sign in and get started

- Use Continue with Google or Email me a sign-in link on the welcome page.

- For email, open the newest link sent to the same address you entered.

- The public pilot welcomes new members. Your first verified sign-in creates your account.

- The account button opens Your account; Sign out ends this browser session.

- Set your public display name in My Profile & Activity.

- Use the profile avatar to return there.

### My Feed: tabs, search, and sorting

- All reactions shows the catalog subject to your filters.

- New today shows videos newly cataloged today using your browser’s local date, not necessarily uploaded today.

- Following shows matches to your account’s follows.

- Favorites shows individual videos you starred.

- Community finds shows catalog matches added through accepted community submissions.

- Search a song, reaction, or reactor searches titles, channel names, and known song names.

- Format offers All formats, Full-length, or Shorts; review status offers Confirmed or Probable.

- Choosing Shorts overrides Hide Shorts for the current view; choosing Probable overrides Only confirmed reactions. Other filters still apply.

- Confirmed is a metadata or moderator classification, not a guarantee that someone watched the video.

- Sort by Newest uploads, Highest rated, Highest ranked reactors, Most viewed, Most discussed, Reactor size, Recently discovered, or Newest reactor.

- Unknown statistics cannot satisfy numeric filters.

- Load more reactions expands the visible list.

- Catalog counts describe the loaded catalog; the filtered result count describes your current view.

### Reaction cards, watch lists, ratings, and comments

- Open a reaction’s discussion to view its details, rate it, comment, or request removal. Report an incorrect Short/full-length designation using Other and explain the correction.

- Open the YouTube link to watch at its source; preview playback follows YouTube and browser rules.

- Use the watch-status selector to track a video’s status and the star to add or remove a Favorite.

- Favorites and Following are different: a Favorite saves an individual video; a follow selects future matching uploads.

- Rate 1–5 for reaction quality and insight, then Save rating.

- Every member’s rating has equal weight; changing your rating does not earn more points.

- Submit comment for review sends your comment to moderators before public display.

- Reply starts a response; Edit resubmits an existing comment for review; Delete removes your own contribution.

- Like, Block, and Report appear where available.

- Blocking hides that member’s content for you.

- For Request video removal, select Wrong performer, Not a reaction, Short or excerpt, Video unavailable, Duplicate listing, or Other relevance issue; explain the problem and Send removal request.

- The listing remains until a moderator acts.

- Disagreement or a low rating alone is not grounds for removal.

### Reactor ratings

- Open Reactors to rate a channel from 1 to 100; 100 is highest.

- Each member has one editable rating per channel. Community score is the average; rating counts show how many members contributed.

- Sort Reactors by Highest ranked, subscribers, or name.

- In My Feed choose Highest ranked reactors to sort videos by their channels’ community scores.

- Clear removes your vote. Reactor ratings earn no participation points and are separate from video ratings.

### Hide reactors

- Select Hide reactor on a reaction card or Hide in Reactors to remove that channel from your personal feed and searches.

- Hidden reactors also stay out of Following, favorites and saved feed views.

- Open Reactors → Hidden reactors and select Unhide to restore visibility.

- This account preference does not remove videos for other members or change your follows and watch history.

### Suggest a reactor or recommend removal

- Open Reactors and use Suggest a reactor or recommend removal. Recommend removal beside a listed channel fills in its details.

- For an addition, supply a UC channel ID or a direct youtube.com/channel/UC… URL, its name, and an explanation with a Missioned Souls reaction link. Channel handles are not accepted in this form.

- Select Send request. My recent requests shows your latest requests, status, and any moderator notes. Up to five requests per day are allowed; duplicate pending requests are blocked.

- A request does not immediately add, remove, or hide a channel. Use Hide for your own feed preferences.

### Following and Reactors

Use **Reactors → Import YouTube subscriptions → Connect YouTube** to grant optional read-only permission. Review matching reactors and select **Follow selected reactors**. Existing follows and hidden reactors are preserved. This is a one-time import; new YouTube subscriptions require another import. If import is awaiting Google configuration, manual follows remain available.

- In Reactors, search channel names, compare community scores, open the channel on YouTube, and click + Follow or Following to toggle a channel follow.

- Following lists performer, reactor, and song follows; Unfollow removes one.

- Enter a song title and click Follow song; matching uses text in the known song name or video title and can include similarly named songs.

- View followed reactions opens My Feed’s Following tab.

- The Following includes selector defaults to followed reactors and songs when you have them; if none exist, performer follows apply.

- All follows also includes every reaction to a followed performer.

- Because this service covers Missioned Souls, following the performer alone includes its whole catalog.

- Other feed filters still apply.

- The Follow performer button toggles Missioned Souls; Manage follows opens the Following page when All performers is selected.

### Saved Views

- Save current feed or Save as new view opens the naming dialog.

- Enter a descriptive name and Save view, or Cancel.

- Open view restores its filters and layout.

- Make default selects the saved view for future visits; Delete removes the saved view without deleting videos, favorites, or follows.

- Saved views save settings, not a frozen copy of results: newly cataloged matching videos can appear later.

### Appearance & preferences / Customize feed

- Both buttons open Make it yours.

- Changes preview immediately.

- Save as default writes your preferences to your account for other devices.

- Save as new view stores a named combination; Close dismisses the drawer and is not a Save button.

- Appearance includes Theme (light/dark/system), Accent (teal/blue/violet), Feed layout (cards/compact/large/table), Density (compact/standard/spacious), Thumbnail size, and Cards per row (2–5).

- Organize your feed includes Group by (none/performer/reactor/song/genre/date/source), Default landing view (All reactions/New today/Favorites/Following/Community finds), Default saved view, Uploaded in the past, From date, Through date, Minimum subscribers, Minimum rating, and Reactor/Song/Genre contains.

- Hide Shorts, Hide watched videos, Only confirmed reactions, and Only followed reactors are additional filters.

- Only followed reactors applies even outside Following.

- Detail toggles select Upload date, Community rating, Reactor subscribers, Views, Likes, Comment count, Performer, Song, Genre, and Discovery date.

- Autoplay requests automatic preview playback; browser restrictions can prevent it.

- A default saved view can override other default settings when opened.

- The Following includes choice is a temporary browsing choice, not currently stored with saved preferences.

### Chat Rooms and Mentions

- Choose a room to open its messages.

- Send message posts the text you entered; use @ followed by a member’s full display name for a mention.

- Messages refresh approximately every five seconds while the chat page is active and visible.

- Room badges show unread activity; the active count reflects recent presence, not total membership.

- Chat does not earn contribution points.

- Own-message Edit/Delete and other-message Reply/Like/Block/Report controls appear when applicable.

- Mentions collects matching chat and comment mentions; Open conversation opens the room or video discussion.

- No personal information, harassment, or spam.

- Being muted prevents posting until the restriction expires or a moderator removes it.

### My Profile & Activity and submitting reactions

- Save display name updates the public name used on contributions and leaderboards.

- Submit a reaction opens the missing-video form.

- Paste a direct YouTube video URL, choose Missioned Souls, explain why it belongs, and click Suggest video.

- Submissions await moderator verification; duplicates, already pending records, and exclusions can be rejected.

- Your contributions and review decisions shows outcomes and moderator notes; Your recent points shows awards.

- Performer recommendations are closed because Reaction Journey is dedicated to Missioned Souls.

### Points & Reputation

- First rating of a video earns +1, up to 10 awards/day.

- Approved comments earn +3, up to 3/day.

- Accepted new reaction submissions earn +20, up to 5/day.

- The first confirmed relevance correction earns +5, up to 5/day.

- Daily award limits reset at midnight UTC; reviewed contributions count on the approval date.

- Duplicate discoveries, unconfirmed reports, rating changes, chat, and moderator self-approvals do not earn additional points.

- Hidden/reversed contributions can reverse their awards.

- Contributor starts at 25 points, Reaction scout at 100, Catalog curator at 250, and Community champion at 1,000.

- Badges confer recognition, not moderator permissions; points have no cash value.

- The page shows your history, total, and community leaderboard.

### Discovery status & catalog coverage

- Expand this panel in My Feed to see the last discovery run, recent batches, recent-upload coverage, and history backlog.

- Channels checked in the past 24 hours measures successful recent-upload checks among eligible Missioned Souls channels; it is a rolling window and can fall when older checks expire.

- Historical pages are separate and do not count as fresh coverage.

- Channels held outside routine discovery retain their records. Confirm Missioned Souls reaction evidence before approving a pending match; approval can enable an ordinary held channel. Paused, retired, and official channels retain their protected scope.

- Recent failures, retry times, channel scopes, and discovery-delay samples help explain incomplete coverage. The last run is not proof of a full catalog cycle.

- A succeeded run completed its allocated work, not the entire catalog.

- A partial run can still check channels and add videos while retaining history or stopping at a budget.

- Newly cataloged IDs are newly stored video IDs; their upload dates may be old.

- Recent/history/performer-search counters identify their sources.

- API calls count request attempts, not Google’s definitive quota usage.

- Elapsed time is wall time; summed API and database timings can overlap and should not be added to estimate duration.

- Uploads examined measures processing work, not new reactions.

- Imported statistics are dated snapshots, not real-time YouTube analytics.

- Scheduled checks and YouTube notifications complement each other; neither guarantees immediate or complete discovery.

### Troubleshooting and support

- If a view is empty, clear search/date/numeric filters, check Hide Shorts and Only confirmed reactions, and verify your follows and Reactors → Hidden reactors. Also try All reactions with All formats and clear the review-status filter.

- If Following shows everything, check whether Missioned Souls is your only follow or whether All follows is selected.

- If a favorite is missing, check Favorites and hidden reactors. A pending, rejected, excluded, or unavailable catalog record will not appear simply because its title was corrected. Contact a moderator with the direct video URL.

- After an update, hard refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows.

- If sign-in loops, use the latest email link and ask the administrator to check site/redirect URLs.

- For Catalog, Social service, or Performer management temporarily unavailable, retry later and report the exact message and time to the administrator.

- A database quota error cannot be fixed by a browser refresh.

- If discovery is stale, an administrator should inspect scheduled runs, secrets, and database limits.

- YouTube Terms opens YouTube’s terms.

- Reaction Journey is an independent fan community; no affiliation is implied.

### Correct video information and restore a missing video

- Open Moderator → Video Classification Review. Correct video information is at the top and works before or after approval, even when a saved video is absent from the member feed.

- Paste its YouTube URL or video ID and select Load video. Check Catalog status, availability, and any Exclusion reason. This tool edits an existing saved video; use submissions or reconciliation for a missing catalog ID.

- Select Fetch YouTube details to populate the title, channel name, channel ID, and upload date. Verify the video on YouTube, choose Short, Full-length, or Unknown, and enter a correction reason.

- Save Corrections changes the details and format while preserving the existing review decision and exclusions. It does not automatically publish a rejected or excluded match.

- After verifying a valid Missioned Souls reaction, select Save Corrections & Restore to deliberately remove its Missioned Souls exclusion and set its match to Confirmed. Restoration requires an existing Missioned Souls match, an available video, and active performer coverage.

- Restoration retains comments, ratings, and other performers’ decisions. Recent corrections records the reason; the audit stores the before and after details.

- Reload the video by ID to verify the result. If it still does not appear, clear feed filters and unhide the correct reactor. Changing the channel moves the video to its corrected channel; channel discovery holds and unavailable-video protections remain separate.

### Approve member-submitted reactions

- Open Moderator Queue and find the community submission in the review queue. Select Fetch YouTube details to fill its title, channel, upload date, and suggested format.

- Open Verify on YouTube to confirm the performer and reaction. Choose Short, Full-length, or Unknown / needs format review; short duration alone does not prove a YouTube Short.

- Enter a review note, choose Accept, and select Save review. Accepted verified submissions are Confirmed; the selected format is saved. Protected exclusions or unavailable videos can prevent publication.

- If fetching fails, expand Video details for the manual fallback and supply the correct title, channel name, and UC channel ID. Check the result message and member feed after approval.

- Use Correct video information for later title or channel corrections and deliberate restoration.

### Import a master workbook

- Open Moderator → Import Workbook and choose a reconciled .xlsx master smaller than 10 MB. Updated Master supplies channels; the newest Video Import YYYY-MM-DD sheet supplies videos.

- Select Preview import. Review new records, already present matches, excluded / rejected matches, protected records, and items needing review. Previewing does not change the catalog.

- Select Import new records to apply missing records after a fresh comparison with the live catalog. Existing decisions and exclusions remain protected; uncertain new records are Pending for moderator review.

- Read or Download receipt and use Recent imports to review previous receipts. Repeating an applied import returns its receipt; uploading again skips existing IDs.

- Previews expire after 24 hours. Re-upload for a fresh preview. Unsupported layouts and conflicting IDs produce an error; identical duplicate rows are collapsed. The limit is 1,000 channels and 1,000 videos.

- Imports preserve existing titles, formats, comments, ratings, account data, availability, and historical scan progress. Deploying the import tool does not automatically import a workbook.

### Reconcile missing reaction video IDs

- In Import Workbook, open Reconcile missing reaction videos. Paste up to 100 video ID,channel ID pairs, one pair per line.

- Load 74 audited missing IDs loads candidates from the October 5 audit. These are candidates to check, not 74 verified reactions or a guarantee that all remain missing.

- Select Check and reconcile to fetch current public YouTube details. Clear matches become Confirmed; known performer matches with uncertain reaction evidence or possible Shorts become Pending.

- Existing matches, exclusions, unavailable videos, channel holds, and ID conflicts are preserved. Unrelated or nonpublic entries are recorded without publication; description-only uncertain matches remain in the receipt for investigation.

- Inspect and download the receipt. Repeat safely by exact, case-sensitive video ID. If an interrupted request has no completed receipt, retry and inspect its result.

- This checks explicit video IDs. It does not reconstruct historical reaction totals or reset history cursors. Use Correct video information for an existing excluded or rejected match that should deliberately be restored.

### User Activity

- Open Moderator → User Activity for timestamped summaries of member actions.

- Search by display name or member ID, filter by activity type, or click a member to focus the list.

- Use Show all users to clear the member selection and Refresh to get new entries.

- The page shows the last 30 days of recorded actions and recent site-presence check-ins. Video clicks distinguish discussion, preview, and YouTube-link actions. Clicking a video or selecting a watch status does not verify playback or completion. Presence reflects recent visible-site check-ins, not continuous attendance.

### Channel Update

- Open Moderator → Channel Update to search channels or filter Unavailable channels only.

- Use Edit name / status to pause, retire or reactivate a channel; save a reason.

- Queue retry makes an active channel eligible for a fresh check in a scheduled run.

- Add channels using a verified UC channel ID or direct /channel/ URL. Use Official performer for Missioned Souls; its performances are not scanned as reactor uploads.

- Retired records and existing videos are retained. Video availability and exclusion decisions are managed separately.

### Contact Inbox

- Open Contact Inbox to read member questions and site issues.

- Select Read / reply, write a response, and send it.

- Close resolved requests or reopen them. A member follow-up reopens a closed request.

- Replies are private within the site; no email is sent.

### Classification and member review

- Open Video Classification Review for the Unknown format and Probable reaction lists.

- Check the video on YouTube, select Short or Full-length, tick the verification box, and confirm.

- Use Member Review under Moderator to search names or email, correct display names, adjust points with a reason, or mute and unmute posting.

- New members are Novice until 25 points, then Contributor. Higher badges follow the existing point thresholds.

## Moderator & administrator help

### Moderator Queue: review safely

- This menu is available only to designated moderators.

- Refresh reloads uncertain matches, submissions, content reports, and performer settings.

- Open the YouTube link and verify Missioned Souls and the reaction’s actual format before deciding.

- Approve publishes the selected eligible match; Exclude rejects that match and records its exclusion.

- Open a published video’s discussion to use Moderator: video format. Select Short, Full-length, or Unknown / needs review, then Save format. Exclude video removes an irrelevant match separately. These controls are available only to moderators. Mark as Short classifies and locks its format; it does not publish or exclude the match. Approve a relevant pending Short separately, or choose Exclude.

- Previously excluded Shorts lists records excluded by the old Mark as Short action. Check each video, then use Restore & approve as Short to deliberately publish a relevant one. Other exclusions remain protected. It does not delete the video from YouTube.

- Select visible (up to 50) selects visible review items; Approve selected, Exclude selected, and Mark selected as Short apply explicit decisions.

- Individual and bulk actions can differ from the global strong-preview publication action.

- Notes and review history remain recorded.

### Recheck previews and strong matches

- Start recheck previews enables background metadata rechecks and starts a batch.

- Recheck next batch now processes another batch; Pause rechecks stops background preview work.

- Previewing alone never publishes or removes videos.

- Review group filters All groups, Strong matches, Possible Shorts / excerpts, Possibly unrelated, Uncertain evidence, Moderator / performer holds, or Not rechecked yet.

- Strong matches require a known reactor, exact performer evidence, reaction wording, and duration over three minutes.

- Metadata cannot prove video contents.

- Publish up to 50 strong preview matches applies up to 50 fresh eligible strong results across the queue, not just checked rows or the visible page.

- It preserves exclusions and protected decisions.

- Preview eligibility can expire or change; a zero result means no eligible current matches.

- Shorts/unrelated suggestions remain for explicit review.

### Missioned Souls discovery settings

- Settings opens the Missioned Souls profile.

- Preserve its canonical name.

- Official channel/website identifies the performer; Alternate names permits up to four verified aliases, one per line.

- Broad aliases can create false matches.

- Initial history scan offers 30 days, 90 days, one year, or older history; changing aliases or the window starts a fresh search.

- Review policy either holds matches or publishes clear full-length reactions automatically; ambiguous/short matches remain for review.

- Enable ongoing discovery controls new discovery for Missioned Souls.

- The chat option controls its published fan room.

- Coverage status Draft hides coverage; Published makes it available to members.

- Save settings stores changes; Save & find reactions also starts a search.

- Find reactions runs discovery for the profile, subject to quota and run budgets.

- Review results opens up to 100 uncertain candidates; Select up to 50 awaiting review and the selected-action buttons apply explicit decisions.

- Other performers cannot be added in this Missioned Souls-only service.

### Submissions, comments, content reports, and mutes

- Refresh review queue reloads community submissions, comments/replies, and relevance reports.

- Accept or reject with an explanatory note after checking evidence.

- Accepting a verified removal report excludes the catalog match; accepted verified new submissions publish a Confirmed match when eligible; choose their format during approval.

- Relevant respectful criticism is allowed regardless of its rating.

- Content reports & chat moderation offers Dismiss report (no content removal), Hide content, or Hide & mute 24h.

- Temporary mutes lists current restrictions; Unmute removes one.

- Moderators should not approve their own submissions for points.

- Keep existing exclusions unless there is a deliberate, supported correction.

### Administrator configuration and deployment

- In Cloudflare, the reactionjourney Worker uses D1 binding DB for reaction-signal-catalog.

- The scheduled trigger is */15 * * * * (every 15 minutes).

- YOUTUBE_API_KEY must be a secret; never paste it into public source, chat, or screenshots.

- SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY configure authentication; never substitute a privileged service-role key in browser configuration.

- COMMUNITY_MODERATOR_EMAILS designates moderator email addresses; MEMBER_EMAIL_ALLOWLIST restricts invited access when configured.

- YOUTUBE_PUSH_CALLBACK_URL must be the public website origin followed by /api/youtube/push.

- Connect / renew channel notifications renews channel subscriptions; received notifications still require API checks and matching.

- DISCOVERY_DAILY_SEARCH_LIMIT currently limits performer searches to 20/day; other API calls and other scripts using the Google project also consume applicable quota.

- Google Cloud’s YouTube Data API quota page is the source for actual quota usage.

- In Supabase Authentication, maintain the Site URL and permitted redirect URLs and enabled email/Google providers.

- Domain changes require checking these settings and the callback URL.

- Current GitHub deployment builds with npm run build and deploys with npm run deploy; the deploy script applies D1 migrations before publishing.

- Keep applied migrations, add new ones, and inspect the build log before hard refreshing. npm run build must produce dist/server/index.js. For a manual preview, use npm run deploy:preview so the Worker is built before preview upload.

- Cloudflare database metrics show row reads/writes; database quota, worker capacity, YouTube quota, and sign-in provider limits are separate checks.

- A successful deployment does not guarantee healthy discovery: verify recent runs, catalog loading, login, and moderation.

## Automatic subscriber refresh

Reactor subscriber counts refresh from YouTube automatically, using the existing 15-minute Cloudflare schedule. Each invocation updates up to 50 channels whose last successful update is at least 24 hours old. The first refresh of 500 channels takes roughly 2½ hours and normally uses 10 YouTube API requests. Opening pages does not make additional YouTube API requests.

The Reactors table shows subscribers and the last successful update date. YouTube supplies rounded public counts; hidden or missing counts display as unknown. Missing channels retain their last known counts and show an unavailable notice. API failures preserve previous data and retry after at least one hour; channels missing from a successful response retry after a day. Moderator names, decisions, exclusions, follows and ratings are preserved.

Discovery status also shows the last subscriber-refresh batch. Refresh requests are included in the existing endpoint request counters. No additional cron configuration or database migration is required. The scheduler must be active and the existing YouTube API key must be configured.
