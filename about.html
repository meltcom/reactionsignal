About Reaction Journey

Reactions and the band’s own journey

An independent fan community for discovering Missioned Souls reactions, following reactors and songs, and sharing the music. My Feed can show reactions and clearly labeled official YouTube uploads. MS Journey separately organizes the band’s videos by year, official playlist, and collection, with links to YouTube, Facebook, and other listed sources.

Your feed, your choices

Search and filter videos, include or hide Shorts, save Favorites and watch status, and keep reusable Saved Views. Most viewed ranks reactions only. Newest reactor and Highest ranked reactors each show one latest matching reaction per channel, ordered by the directory’s newest criteria or community reactor score.

Follow individual reactors or all matching reactors across the directory. Save private Following groups, switch by choosing a group, and open your followed reactions on My Feed. Discuss and rate videos or reactors, join fan chats, suggest missing reactions, and earn Contribution Points for helpful contributions.

Scheduled discovery and channel notifications help grow the catalog. Coverage may be incomplete; recorded counts can lag current totals. Automated matching does not verify video contents. Videos remain hosted by their original platforms.

Optional: import YouTube subscriptions

After signing in, expand Reactors → Import YouTube subscriptions. Connect YouTube with separate read-only Google permission, review matching catalog reactors in batches of up to 100, and confirm the selections you want to follow here. You can check for new matches later or finish after any batch.

Subscription reads happen on request; we do not run background imports or change your YouTube subscriptions. The server stores an encrypted Google refresh token to check permission daily and before imports. The browser receives channel IDs for review, not Google tokens. Only confirmed reactor IDs are retained as tracked imported follows; the full subscription list is not saved.

Manage YouTube connection lets you disconnect, revoke access, and delete tracked imported follows. Manual follows, ratings, and Favorites are retained. Older imports made before tracking require manual review. Saved groups exclude tracked imported follows. You can use the service without granting YouTube access; Google sign-in does not import subscriptions.

Read the Privacy Policy for access, storage, retention, revocation, and deletion details.

Who operates Reaction Journey?

Reaction Journey is operated by its independent fan-community administrator. It is not affiliated with Missioned Souls, Google, YouTube, or listed reactor channels. Questions: contact@reactionjourney.com. Support through the PayPal link is voluntary.
