Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Last updated October 6, 2026

Reaction Journey is an independent Missioned Souls fan community using YouTube API Services.

Information and use

We store your account ID, email, display name, saved choices (follows, hidden reactors, favorites, watch status, ratings, preferences and views), community posts, suggestions, reports, points and support messages. We record site presence, video-link/preview clicks and summaries of member actions. We use this information for sign-in, personalized feeds, community features, support, moderation and security.

Other members can see published community content and display names. Moderators can review member activity; support messages are private to you and moderators.

YouTube import

With your separate youtube.readonly consent, we read the selected YouTube account/channel’s subscriptions to find matching reactors. The full list and temporary Google access token stay in browser memory; our server receives only channel IDs you confirm as follows. These follows and their activity records are stored. We do not retain the Google token, change your YouTube subscriptions or perform background imports.

You can unfollow reactors or revoke access in Google security permissions. Revocation does not automatically remove stored follows; contact us to delete imported information and related activity records.

Providers and browser storage

Cloudflare hosts the site, database and operational logs; Supabase handles authentication, including email or Google sign-in. They process information needed for those services, including account and technical data such as IP addresses and request details. Browser storage keeps sessions and preferences. Google and YouTube receive requests for sign-in, imports, thumbnails and playback; their content may use cookies, measurement and advertising. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information, use Google user data for targeted advertising, or train generalized AI models with imported subscriptions. Information may be disclosed when legally required or needed to address security or abuse.

Retention and your choices

Records are retained while needed for the service, support, moderation or legal obligations. There is no automatic expiry for all member records. Backups and provider logs follow their retention periods. HTTPS and access controls protect member information, but no system is completely secure.

For access, correction, export, deletion or privacy questions, email contact@reactionjourney.com, preferably from your account email, or use Contact while signed in. Requests are handled manually after identity verification; we explain any required retention. Deleting this account does not delete your Google or YouTube account.

The service is for ages 13+. Contact us about information supplied by a child under 13. Material changes to data use will receive appropriate notice and consent.

Reaction Journey is an independent fan community for Missioned Souls. It is not affiliated with Google, YouTube, or the artists and reactor channels it catalogs.

Questions: contact@reactionjourney.com
