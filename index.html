    Add a band or performer

    1. Identify the performer

    Use up to four alternate names. Each one is searched and matched against video titles. Changing names or the history window starts a fresh search.

    2. Choose discovery options

    Short or ambiguous matches always await review. Automatic matching uses titles and duration; it does not watch the video.

    3. Choose visibility

    Start with a draft. Review results, then publish when you’re ready. Turning discovery off pauses scans while keeping published coverage available.
